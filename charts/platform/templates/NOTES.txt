  scarab      the per-workspace brokers
  pestilence  the control plane
  town        the management interface

Before anyone can sign in, two things must exist. Both are cluster-specific, so they
are scripts rather than chart values:

  town's cluster-setup-scripts/generate-keys.sh    the assertion key pair
  town's cluster-setup-scripts/create-secrets.sh   the GitHub client and session secrets

Check what is running:

  kubectl get pods -n town -n pestilence -n scarab
